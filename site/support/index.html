About that blue box

"Windows protected your PC"

Windows shows this for any program it hasn't seen many people download. CashDesk is new and built just for my clients, so Windows doesn't know it yet. It isn't a sign that anything is wrong.

Click More info, then Run anyway. If you're unsure, stop and call me. I'm happy to stay on the line.

On Ubuntu or Debian CASHDESK-X86_64.DEB

  1. Download the installer. It lands in your Downloads folder.
  2. Open a terminal and run sudo apt install ./Downloads/CashDesk-x86_64.deb, then type your password.
  3. Open CashDesk from your apps and read me the ID and one-time password it shows.
Download for Ubuntu

On an Android phone or tablet CASHDESK.APK

  1. Download the app in your phone's browser and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open CashDesk and read me the ID and one-time password. When I connect, tap Accept and allow screen sharing when Android asks.

You stay in charge

Nothing happens without you

CashDesk is a remote-support tool, not a back door. Here's what that means in practice.

Only with your code

I can't connect unless you give me the ID and password shown on your screen. The password changes every time.

You see everything

Every click I make happens on your screen in front of you. Ask me what I'm doing at any point.

End it any time

Click Disconnect or close CashDesk and the session ends at once. You can uninstall it like any other app.

A private connection

The connection is encrypted and runs through my own server, not a third-party support company.

Questions people ask

Does it cost anything?

No. CashDesk is free for my clients. You only pay for the support work itself, as we agree on the phone.

Can you help when I'm not at the computer?

For regular clients, I can set CashDesk up with a permanent password so I can do maintenance without you at the keyboard. I'll only do that with your OK, and you can remove it any time.

I'm on a Mac, iPhone or iPad.

Mac and iPhone/iPad aren't supported yet. CashDesk runs on Windows, Ubuntu and Android. Call me and we'll find another way to get you sorted.

Someone else called claiming to be me.

I'll only ever send you to cashconsults.com for CashDesk, and I'll be on the phone with you when you use it. If in doubt, hang up and call me on 704‑292‑6994.
